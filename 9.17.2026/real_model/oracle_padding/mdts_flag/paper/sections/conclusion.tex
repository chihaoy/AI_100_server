\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Ahead-of-time compilation fixes, before routing is known, how many rows each expert computes and how its outputs return
to their tokens. \sys adapts the first through data, binding compiled capacity tiers to per-device load ranks, and keeps the
second efficient with an aggregation graph that preserves device ownership and token-local reduction. The two must be
designed together: ranked tiers cut the latency of 512-token chunks by 27\% with this aggregation but by 4\% with a
repaired dense combine. On four Cloud AI 100 SoCs, \sys prefills Qwen3-30B-A3B 3.65--4.74$\times$ faster than the production
compilation. Selecting the tier structure from measured stage costs, and other models and compilers, come next.
